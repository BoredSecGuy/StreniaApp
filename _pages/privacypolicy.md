# Strenia Privacy Policy

## Overview

Strenia is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your personal information is collected, used, and disclosed by our fitness tracking application.

## Information We Collect

### Personal Information

- **Authentication Data**: Apple ID information when you sign in with Apple
- **User Profile**: Name and basic profile information you provide

### Workout Data

- Exercise records, sets, repetitions, and weights
- Personal records and progress metrics
- Workout history and routines

## How We Use Your Information

- To provide and maintain the Strenia app functionality
- To create and manage your personal workout profile
- To track your fitness progress and achievements
- To back up your data via iCloud (when enabled)

## Data Storage and Security

- Your workout data is stored locally on your device
- When iCloud backup is enabled, your data is securely stored in your personal iCloud account
- We implement appropriate security measures to protect your personal information

## Data Sharing

- **We do not sell your personal data to third parties**
- Your data is not shared with advertisers or marketing companies
- We do not use your workout data for analytics purposes

## Third-Party Services

- Apple SignIn for authentication
- Apple StoreKit for in-app purchases
- Apple iCloud for optional data backup

## Your Choices and Rights

- You may opt out of iCloud backup at any time
- You can delete your account and all associated data
- You have the right to access and export your workout data

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the app.
